Story

"Through discussion and the exchange of ideas, we adapt and grow. We are a small, colorful studio that values and respects both our own abilities and those of others, even if we come from different backgrounds and institutions. Yet, we share the same ideal: creating art through diverse techniques, pushing each other forward, under the belief that 'art is a story with no end.'"
